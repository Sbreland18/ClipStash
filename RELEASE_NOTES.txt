Version 1.4.0

- Subtitles / transcript only: a new quality option that fetches just the
  captions, with no video at all. A transcript is a few kilobytes instead
  of a few hundred megabytes
- Split at chapters: turns a long video with chapter markers into one
  file per chapter
- Warns when you queue something you have downloaded before, and says
  when you got it
- Checks about once a week for a newer yt-dlp, so you hear about a fix
  before downloads start failing rather than after
- Start at: hold a queue until a set time, for running large batches
  outside busy hours
