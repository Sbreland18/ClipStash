Version 1.2.0

- Copy a YouTube link while 'Watch clipboard' is on and it queues itself
- History window showing everything downloaded and where it was saved
- Warns before starting if a queue looks likely to fill the drive
- Double-click a queue row to open the folder its files went to
- A summary at the end of a queue when something didn't work, with the
  option to retry there and then
- Unexpected errors now show a plain explanation and are written to the
  log, instead of a technical error box
